# SynBioCrow 2.4.19 — Development Failure-Mode Decomposition

The corrected 2.4.18.1 audit shows heterogeneous failure modes rather than one global problem. This stage freezes both generation and the 2.4.16 ordering and classifies each development target using two transparent diagnostic axes: best target-excluded internal-anchor similarity and the frozen rank of that best near-miss candidate.

Diagnostic thresholds are intentionally simple and are not fitted: mean internal-anchor similarity >=0.45 denotes a meaningful structural near miss; rank <=50 denotes high placement. The four resulting classes separate candidate-space coverage from ranking placement. These labels are development diagnostics, not validation claims and not lifecycle certification.

No validation/evaluation truth is accessed. Bakuchiol and D-allitol remain sealed.
