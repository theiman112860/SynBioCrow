# SynBioCrow 2.4.23 — Score-Component Counterfactual Audit

2.4.22 shows target-specific suppression. Curcumin's best internal-anchor near miss is most conspicuously disadvantaged on precedent-step fraction, while jasmonic acid is disadvantaged on both step-feasibility mean and precedent coverage. Saturated rule/template/domain/type coverage contributes little pairwise discrimination.

2.4.23 tests whether persisted features are actually coupled to the frozen 2.4.16 coverage-adjusted score before another ranking policy is attempted. For each ranking-limited development target, it measures feature-versus-score association and performs one-component-at-a-time OLS residualization. The rank of the best corrected internal-anchor candidate under each counterfactual is reported.

This is causal-diagnostic in the limited score-component sense, not a new production ranker. No counterfactual is adopted automatically. Validation/evaluation truth remains sealed.
