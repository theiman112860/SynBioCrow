# Limitations

- Candidate constructs are computational designs, not experimentally certified constructs.
- Specialized BioPKS pathways are Candidate only.
- `rule0024_21` lacks exact independent direct-reaction evidence, verified enzyme identity, and quantitative thermodynamics.
- Five bounded BioPKS executions were no-hits at the tested depth; this is not proof that no pathway exists.
- The release does not claim expression, activity, flux, toxicity, manufacturability, or experimental viability.
