# SynBioCrow 2.2 paper draft — Results

## Consolidation
The 2.2 RC integrates four generator families behind a shared Candidate contract and reaction-level ensemble graph.

## Composite-route capability
Hermetic tests recover target-to-sink routes assembled from reaction edges contributed by independent backends.

## Evidence behavior
PASS/FAIL/ABSTAIN semantics prevent contextual hits/model scores from masquerading as exact reaction/enzyme evidence.

## Construct behavior
The digital Build layer verifies CDS-to-protein identity, preserves amino-acid sequence during synonymous redesign, applies deterministic QC, and produces Candidate cassettes.

## DBTL behavior
Structured Test outcomes update versioned ranking policy through bounded deterministic updates with no lifecycle effect.

## Release verification
Final CI counts, runtime matrix, package size, and SHA-256 values should be inserted from the tagged RC workflow.
